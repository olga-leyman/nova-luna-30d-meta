Takeaway

Scale Microneedling, PRX, and Letybo Instant Forms. Keep the wedding quiz on, with a harder LA geo — it produced two of the five clients. Keep Treatment Match offer live as the DM and client lane — that is where Josefina and lei sales came from. Recycle Event Timing and Acne as Reels. Pause HIFU Face and Botox Stories.
