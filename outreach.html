What to tell the room

  1. The three-channel twice motion is only half-built. Call + SMS + voicemail is the real bundle. Email is missing (3 people). 20 calls is missing (max is 9, median is 2).
  2. Speed is the hole, not effort on Instant Forms. 48% same-calendar-day first touch. Zero Saturdays. Friday leads wait until Monday. Sep 10 was 48 people in one blast.
  3. 33 leads got the clean twice. 42 got a 5–11 day chase with an empty inbox. After two silent triples, stop or change the message — do not add a ninth voicemail.
  4. The inbox is the queue. Phoenix called back and still got five more blast days. Lucine, Amanda, Shaid, Jeannie already wrote — those names should jump the list, not sit in Thursday’s sweep.
  5. Bookings came from 1–4 live days, not from 20 attempts. Brishna booked on one triple. lei sales booked on two. The long chase did not produce the clients.
Next hour of her time is worth more on same-day first connect, and on the people who already wrote back, than on the ninth voicemail to Domenica or Kimberly.
